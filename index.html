Why Tui Torque Motors

A workshop that treats your car like ours.

Tui Torque Motors (trading as Car Master Automotive) is a family-run car servicing workshop in Wiri, Auckland, established in 2020. The workshop services all makes and models — petrol, diesel, and hybrid — offering full car servicing, WOF inspections, wheel alignment with nitrogen inflation, dealer-grade diagnostic scanning, brake and safety work, and fleet maintenance for local businesses. Pricing starts at $60 +GST for a diagnostic scan, $160 +GST for a basic service, $230 +GST for a standard service, and $80 +GST for a four-wheel laser alignment. The workshop is located at Unit 2, 69 Wiri Station Road, Wiri, Auckland 2104, and is open Monday–Friday 9am–5pm, with weekend walk-ins for standard servicing and after-hours appointments available for WOF and repairs. Every job includes a digital service record and a plain-English quote before any work begins.

Since 2020 we've been the local, family-run alternative to the big chains — honest advice, fair pricing, and mechanics who explain the job in plain English before it starts.

Common questions

Straight answers,
no fine print.

How much does a Warrant of Fitness (WOF) cost in Wiri?

WOF inspections are available by appointment, alongside our standard servicing and repair work. Call or text 022 095 0555 for current WOF pricing.

How much does a car service cost in Auckland?

A Basic Car Service (oil & filter change, multi-point safety check, fluids topped up, digital service record) starts at $160 +GST. A Standard Service, which adds a brake & tyre inspection and battery health test, starts at $230 +GST.

Do you service hybrid vehicles?

Yes. We service all makes and models, including petrol, diesel and hybrid vehicles.

How much does a diagnostic scan cost?

A diagnostic scan is $60 +GST and includes a full fault-code read, a plain-English report, and a repair quote.

How much does a wheel alignment cost?

A four-wheel laser alignment starts at $80 +GST, with an optional nitrogen inflation add-on and a steering check included.

What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Walk-ins are welcome on Saturday and Sunday for standard servicing. After-hours appointments, WOF inspections and repairs can be booked in advance.

Can I book a service online?

Yes — enter your number plate, pick a service and leave your contact details on our booking form, and we'll confirm a time by phone or text. You can also call or text 09 869 7579 / 022 095 0555 directly.

Where is Tui Torque Motors / Car Master Automotive located?

We're at Unit 2, 69 Wiri Station Road, Wiri, Auckland 2104.
