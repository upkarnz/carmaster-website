Whether you're lifting a 4x4 for clearance and heavier off-road tyres, or lowering a daily driver with coilovers for a tighter stance, the install and alignment matter as much as the kit itself. We fit lift and lowering kits in-house and follow it with a full alignment and steering check — not an add-on, part of the job.

Lift kits range from 3" to 6" depending on what you're running and what clearance you need. Lowering kits are matched to your car and intended use, from a mild stance drop to a full coilover setup for adjustable ride height.

Bring in your vehicle details and what you're trying to achieve and we'll recommend a kit that fits.

Common questions

Straight answers,
no fine print.

How much lift can I fit on my truck or 4x4?

It depends on the vehicle and what else you're running — bigger tyres, for example. Bring your vehicle in and we'll recommend a kit from 3" up to 6".

Do I need an alignment after a lift or lowering kit?

Yes, always — we include a full alignment and steering check as part of every lift or lowering install, not as a separate charge you have to remember to book.

Will a lift or lowering kit affect my WOF?

Modifications need to meet NZTA/LVVTA standards to pass a WOF. We fit kits designed to meet those standards and can advise if anything needs certification.

Can you fit a kit I've already bought?

In most cases yes — bring the kit and vehicle in and we'll confirm fitment before booking the install.
