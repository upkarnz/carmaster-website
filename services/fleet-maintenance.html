What's included

Ask about fleet rates

  • Scheduled servicing across your whole fleet, tracked so nothing gets missed
  • Priority booking so vehicles are back on the road fast
  • Consolidated invoicing — one invoice, not one per vehicle
  • Digital service records for every vehicle, useful for resale and compliance

Whether it's two vans or a dozen utes, unplanned downtime is the expensive part of running a fleet — not the servicing itself. We work with local Auckland businesses to set up a maintenance schedule that keeps vehicles on the road and off our waiting list, with priority booking when something does need attention.

Instead of chasing separate invoices for every vehicle, fleet customers get consolidated invoicing that makes reconciliation straightforward. Every vehicle gets the same digital service record as our regular customers — useful for resale, warranty claims, and knowing exactly what's been done and when.

Get in touch with your fleet size and vehicle types and we'll put a rate and schedule together.
