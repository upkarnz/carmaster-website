A stock exhaust is a compromise — we build custom mandrel-bent stainless systems to what you actually want, whether that's a noticeable improvement in flow and tone for a performance car, or a quieter, more refined note for a daily driver.

Cat-back systems are the most common performance upgrade, replacing everything from the catalytic converter back for improved flow without touching emissions compliance. We fabricate to fit your specific car rather than working from a generic universal kit, and check clearance and fitment before it leaves the workshop.

Tell us the sound and performance you're after and we'll talk you through what's realistic for your car.

Common questions

Straight answers,
no fine print.

Will a custom exhaust affect my WOF or emissions compliance?

Cat-back systems keep the catalytic converter in place, so they don't affect emissions compliance. We build to meet WOF requirements — ask if you're unsure about a specific setup.

Can you make my exhaust quieter, not louder?

Yes — we build systems for daily-driver refinement as often as we do performance setups. Tell us what you're after.

Do you work with aftermarket exhaust parts I've already bought?

In most cases yes — bring the parts and vehicle in and we'll confirm fitment before booking the fabrication and fitting.

How long does a custom exhaust job take?

It depends on the complexity of the system, but most custom fabrication jobs are completed same-day or next-day. We'll confirm a timeframe when you book.
