WOF Inspections in Wiri, Auckland

A Warrant of Fitness is the legal check that keeps your car road-legal in New Zealand. We run full WOF inspections alongside our regular servicing, so if anything needs fixing to pass, it can usually be sorted the same visit.

What's included

WOF inspections, by appointment

  • Full VINZ-standard safety inspection covering brakes, steering, suspension, tyres, lights and structure
  • Plain-English breakdown of any items that need attention before it'll pass
  • Same-visit repairs where possible, so you're not booking a second appointment
  • Digital record of the inspection and any work carried out

New Zealand cars need a WOF every 6 or 12 months depending on the vehicle's age and registration date — check the sticker on your windscreen if you're not sure when yours is due. We inspect to the same standard as any VINZ-certified testing station, covering everything from brakes and tyres to lights, structure and steering.

If your car doesn't pass first time, we'll walk you through exactly what needs fixing and quote it before touching anything. Because we're also a full servicing workshop, most WOF repair work — brake pads, tyres, lights, minor structural fixes — can be done there and then rather than sending you elsewhere and starting the process again.

We're open Monday to Friday 9am–5pm for booked appointments, with weekend walk-ins available for standard servicing. After-hours WOF appointments can be arranged — call or text 022 095 0555.

How much does a Warrant of Fitness (WOF) cost in Wiri?

WOF inspections are available by appointment, alongside our standard servicing and repair work. Call or text 022 095 0555 for current WOF pricing.

How often does my car need a WOF in New Zealand?

Vehicles first registered less than 3 years ago that were new when registered need a WOF every 12 months. Older vehicles need one every 6 months. Check your current WOF label for the exact due date.

What happens if my car fails its WOF?

We'll give you a plain-English list of what needs fixing. Because we're a full workshop, most repairs — brakes, tyres, lights, minor structural items — can be done on the spot so you're not making a second trip.

Can I get a WOF without booking ahead?

WOF inspections are by appointment on weekdays. We do take walk-ins on weekends for standard servicing — call ahead if you'd like us to fit a WOF in as well.
