What's included

Basic Service $160 +GST · Standard Service $230 +GST

  • Oil and filter change with the correct spec oil for your engine

  • Fluids topped up to manufacturer levels
  • Standard Service adds a full brake & tyre inspection plus a battery health test

Basic servicing on Japanese hatchbacks and hybrids like the Prius or Aqua starts from $119 +GST — everyday runabouts don't need the same level of work as a performance European car, so we price accordingly. Premium and European makes — BMW, Audi, Mercedes-Benz and similar — are serviced to manufacturer standard from $230 +GST.

Every service includes a genuine or OEM-equivalent parts quote before we touch anything, so there are no surprise line items on the invoice. We log every job to a digital service record, which matters at resale time and for keeping manufacturer warranties valid.

Not sure whether you need a Basic or Standard service? Tell us your car's make, model and last service date when you book and we'll recommend the right one.

How much does a car service cost in Auckland?

A Basic Car Service (oil & filter change, multi-point safety check, fluids topped up, digital service record) starts at $160 +GST. A Standard Service, which adds a brake & tyre inspection and battery health test, starts at $230 +GST. Basic cars like a Prius or Aqua start from $119 +GST; premium and European makes start from $230 +GST.

Do you service hybrid vehicles?

Yes. We service all makes and models, including petrol, diesel and hybrid vehicles.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use genuine or OEM-equivalent parts and quote them before any work starts, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How do I know if I need a Basic or Standard service?

As a rough guide: if it's been a while since your last brake or tyre check, or your battery is a few years old, Standard Service covers that in the same visit. Tell us your car's history when you book and we'll advise.
