Poor alignment shows up as uneven tyre wear, a car that drifts or pulls on a straight road, or a steering wheel that sits crooked when driving straight. Left unchecked, it wears tyres out faster and can affect handling and braking distance.

We use laser-guided equipment to check and correct all four wheels against manufacturer specifications, not just a front-end toe adjustment. Every alignment includes a steering check, and we can top your tyres up with nitrogen on request — it holds pressure more consistently than regular air as temperatures change.

Alignment is worth checking any time you get new tyres fitted, after hitting a decent pothole or kerb, or if you've noticed uneven wear on your current set.

Common questions

Straight answers,
no fine print.

How much does a wheel alignment cost?

A four-wheel laser alignment starts at $80 +GST, with an optional nitrogen inflation add-on and a steering check included.

How do I know if I need a wheel alignment?

Common signs are uneven tyre wear, the car pulling to one side on a flat road, or the steering wheel sitting off-centre when driving straight.

Should I get an alignment when I buy new tyres?

It's a good idea — new tyres wear evenly from the start if the alignment is correct, so it protects the investment you've just made.

What's the benefit of nitrogen in my tyres?

Nitrogen holds pressure more consistently than regular air as temperatures change through the day, which keeps handling and wear more predictable.
